# Three synthetic personas

These notes are invented for a hackathon demo and for the accuracy spike. They are not real patients. The lab numbers and drug histories are typical of published trial language, not copied from anyone's chart.

Home cities are in the Inclusion region so the on-device distance filter has something to do. Coordinates are city-level only.

## Persona A: Alex Rivera

62-year-old never-smoker in Columbus, Ohio. Stage IV lung adenocarcinoma. EGFR exon 19 deletion. One prior line: osimertinib, with progression. ECOG 1. No brain metastases. PD-L1 TPS 30%. ALK and KRAS not detected. Creatinine 0.9 mg/dL, eGFR 85 mL/min. ANC, platelets and hemoglobin in a normal range.

This is the demo path: a possible match for later-line EGFR or post-TKI studies, and a poor match for first-line, no-prior-EGFR-TKI studies.

## Persona B: Jordan Blake

71-year-old former smoker (40 pack-years, quit 5 years ago) in Cleveland, Ohio. Stage IIIB squamous NSCLC, unresectable. No EGFR, ALK, or KRAS driver reported. PD-L1 TPS 80%. ECOG 2. No prior systemic therapy for this diagnosis. No brain metastases. Creatinine 1.6 mg/dL, eGFR 48 mL/min.

This path is meant to stress ECOG caps of 0-1, squamous histology, high PD-L1, and renal cutoffs.

## Persona C: Sam Okonkwo

54-year-old never-smoker in Cincinnati, Ohio. Stage IV lung adenocarcinoma. ALK rearrangement. Progressed on alectinib (one prior line). Brain metastases treated with stereotactic radiosurgery 6 weeks ago, now stable, off steroids. ECOG 0. EGFR and KRAS not detected. PD-L1 TPS 5%. Organs within normal limits.

This path is meant to stress ALK-positive trials, treated and stable CNS disease, and studies that exclude known drivers.
